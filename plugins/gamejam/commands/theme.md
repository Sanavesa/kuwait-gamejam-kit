argument-hint: the jam theme (e.g. "Echoes") + any direction you're leaning
---
Help me turn the jam theme into strong game directions. The theme is in "$ARGUMENTS" — if that's empty, ask me for it first.

Why this matters: judges score **Theme** (a game that *visibly* fits wins points) and reward a **fresh take** (that's Creativity too). A clear, simple interpretation beats a clever one nobody gets.

Work with me **interactively, in small batches** — don't dump everything at once:

**Step 1 — Unpack the theme.** Restate it in plain words, then map what it could *mean*:
- a **literal** reading, a **metaphorical** one, the **opposite/subversion**, and a **lateral / unexpected** angle;
- the **hard constraint** each angle implies (a constraint focuses the whole design — it's a gift, not a limit).
Then name the **obvious clichés** everyone will make this jam, so I can either *nail one better than anyone* or *deliberately avoid it*.

**Step 2 — Find the hooks.** For 3–4 of the most promising angles, give a one-line **"what if…"** seed and the **single mechanic** it suggests. Favour angles that are *mechanically* interesting, not just thematically cute.

**Step 3 — Make it legibly on-theme.** For my favourite direction, suggest concrete ways the theme can show up in the **mechanic**, the **goal**, the **visuals**, and the **title/framing** — so a judge *gets it within the first 30 seconds*. (Theme expressed through the **mechanic** beats theme as a coat of paint.)

**Step 4 — Hand off.** When I have a direction I like, offer to run **/brainstorm** to develop it into a full, scoped concept (then `/brief` and `/new-game`).

Ask me which angle excites me most and push from there. Keep me from over-thinking — pick a clear take and build.
